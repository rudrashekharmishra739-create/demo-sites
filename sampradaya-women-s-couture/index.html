Questions

How long does custom tailoring take?

Blouses and everyday wear: 3–5 days. Bridal: 3–6 weeks depending on handwork. Rush orders possible - ask.

Do I need to bring my own fabric?

Either way. Choose from the in-store library or bring your own - the quote adjusts accordingly.

What if the fit isn't perfect?

First fitting alterations are part of the process, honoured free within reason.

Do you deliver outside Bengaluru?

Yes - measurements can be taken remotely with guidance, finished garments shipped.
